HalachaBench: what is in this repository

A plain-English map of HalachaBench/halacha-bench-results, the results repository of the HalachaBench project.

Read this first: every number here is provisional. One AI model (itself a contestant) has graded the answers; no rabbi has reviewed the rubrics or graded any answer yet. These scores are not exam grades, do not say whether a model "passes semicha", and are not authority to pasken.

What HalachaBench is

HalachaBench is an independent project that tests how well AI models answer real questions from the semicha (rabbinic ordination) exams of the Chief Rabbinate of Israel. It is not connected to, or endorsed by, the Chief Rabbinate or by any model developer.

The v1 results published here cover 27 models on the test set: the 60 questions of five papers from the Cheshvan 5787 sitting (Niddah, Chuppah v'Kiddushin, Mitzvot HaTeluyot BaAretz, Mikvaot, Tefillah u'Brachot). Each model answered every question three times, in Hebrew, closed-book, and one judge (GPT-6 Astra) graded every answer against sealed rubrics.

What is in the repository

PathWhat it is
README.mdThe main document: full results table for all 27 models, the methodology (questions, answering conditions, rubrics, grading, pre-registration), detailed checks on the Grok results, caveats, and a Hebrew summary.
results.pngBar chart of the 27 models' scores with 95% intervals (shown above).
bundle/criteria.jsonEvery rubric criterion's id, points, axis and status for all 96 sealed questions (60 test + 36 development), without criterion text.
bundle/commitments.json, bundle/seal-log.jsonlSalted cryptographic commitments to all 96 sealed rubrics and the hash-chained log that sealed them on 29 October 2026, before any test answer was graded.
bundle/prereg.jsonSHA-256 of each pre-registration record (questions, exam rules, model list, judge protocol, analysis code).
bundle/scores.jsonThe judge's mean score per model.
bundle/withheld.jsonSHA-256 of the per-answer files (each answer's commitment, every verdict, the 244 repeat gradings), withheld until the rabbi review round closes so blind grading stays blind.
bundle/MANIFEST.jsonSHA-256 of every file, so any later change to the published data can be detected.

Note what is not here: the exam questions themselves (the Chief Rabbinate's own, published on gov.il), the rubric texts and answers (committed to by hash, to be published after the review round), and the runner/analysis code. Until the code, salts and withheld files are published, the bundle fixes the data against later change but cannot be used to recompute a score.

Caveats the authors themselves list

  • The judge, GPT-6 Astra, is also a contestant; the rubric writer, Claude Fable 5.1, placed first. A second judge (Claude Opus 5.5) and rabbi grading are planned.
  • Some models were added after the first publication, each by a recorded pre-registration amendment.
  • Not contamination-free: Rabbinate questions often recur and these 60 have not yet been checked for repeats.
  • Five more pre-registered runs are still to be added, whatever they score.

בעברית, בקצרה

HalachaBench הוא מיזם עצמאי שבודק עד כמה מודלים של בינה מלאכותית יודעים לענות על שאלות אמיתיות ממבחני ההסמכה של הרבנות הראשית לישראל. התוצאות כאן מכסות 27 מודלים על 60 שאלות מחמישה שאלונים ממועד חשוון תשפ"ז; כל מודל ענה שלוש פעמים, בעברית, בלי ספרים ובלי אינטרנט.

התוצאות זמניות: עד כה בדק את התשובות רק שופט ממוחשב אחד, שהוא גם אחד הנבחנים. אף רב עדיין לא עבר על המחוונים או בדק תשובות. הציונים אינם ציון במבחן הסמיכה ואין בהם היתר לסמוך על מודל בפסיקת הלכה.
